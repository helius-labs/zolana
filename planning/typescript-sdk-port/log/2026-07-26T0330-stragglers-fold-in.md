does not raise the variant. Control edit re-run here: unwrapping the call so the
signer's own rejection propagates. Caught by the new `wallet.test.ts` case and
by the producer-disposition scan, which is the pairing that makes the scan
meaningful rather than a partition assertion over hand-written sets.
